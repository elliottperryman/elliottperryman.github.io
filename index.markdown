I work on methods for the design of neutron experiments that exploit physics knowledge and modern statistical methods as part of my PhD at the [Institut Laue-Langevin](https://www.ill.eu), [Paul Scherrer Institut](https://www.psi.ch/en), and [UGA](https://www.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/english/). You’ll find links to my work/results of my work below. I’ve spent the last two years working on Gaussian process models for inelastic neutron scattering.  I’ve also worked on signal processing, distributed optimization for Gaussian processes, and geometry during my undergrad at OakRidge and time in Berkeley.
